NO.001 — THE BLACK HOLE
@sideratlas

Bends light into multiple images of its disk
Approaching gas appears brighter
Gravity redshifts light and delays its arrival
Inner orbits shear bright structures faster

An original calculation inspired by NASA's 2019 visualization, with a non-rotating black hole — Schwarzschild / a* = 0
No NASA footage or code is used

WHAT IS CALCULATED
Schwarzschild light paths, gravitational/Doppler shifts, multiple disk images, foreground occultation and relative photon travel times
Mean brightness follows Page–Thorne; observed total-light intensity is I_obs = g⁴ I_em

WHAT IS ASSUMED
An opaque, razor-thin disk from 6 to 28 r_g, with identical modeled emission on both faces
The view makes a full 360° revolution above and below the disk: a virtual scan with chosen timing, not a physical camera orbit
Moving streaks are a kinematic brightness model, not magnetic turbulence; their mean preserves the radial flux
No physical mass or accretion rate is assigned

Orange is an illustrative BRIGHTNESS palette, not true visible color
The 8-second loop represents 800 GM/c³; periodic source births close the loop without changing orbital speeds
Gas evolves forward throughout; real turbulence need not repeat
Disk thickness, fluid evolution, magnetic fields, a corona and returning radiation are omitted
