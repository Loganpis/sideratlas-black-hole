# Third-party assets and scientific attribution

The bundled assets retain their own licenses. No repository-wide license for the original project code or media is declared in this release.

## Latin Modern fonts

The six unmodified `fonts/lmroman*.otf` files are from Latin Modern 2.005, by Bogusław Jackowski and Janusz M. Nowacki, based on Donald E. Knuth's Computer Modern family. Copyright 2003–2021 by B. Jackowski and J. M. Nowacki on behalf of TeX Users Groups.

They are distributed under the [GUST Font License](fonts/GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt), based on the [LaTeX Project Public License](fonts/LPPL-1.3c.txt). The original [README](fonts/README), asset inventory, and complete upstream distribution links are in [FONT-SOURCES.md](fonts/FONT-SOURCES.md). The bundled subset is unmodified.

`fonts/STIXTwoMath-Regular.otf` is an unmodified asset from the official STIX v2.13b171 release. The original [font log](fonts/STIX-FONTLOG.txt) and [upstream source links](fonts/FONT-SOURCES.md) are included.

## CIE color-matching data

CIE (2019), *Colour-matching functions of CIE 1931 standard colorimetric observer*, International Commission on Illumination, Vienna, AT. [DOI: 10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b](https://doi.org/10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b).

The unmodified CSV and official metadata in `data/` are distributed under [Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), as recorded in the [upstream metadata](data/CIE_xyz_1931_2deg.csv_metadata.json). [Data sources and checksums](data/SOURCES.md) are preserved. These data support earlier spectral-color experiments; the current Kerr–Newman Reel uses illustrative bolometric brightness.

## Scientific references and visual inspiration

The current model's complete bibliography and the role of each study are in [README-KERR-NEWMAN.md](README-KERR-NEWMAN.md). Earlier models have separate references in their respective READMEs. Papers are cited, not redistributed. The numerical implementation is original.

NASA Goddard / Jeremy Schnittman's [2019 black-hole visualization](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/13326/) is credited as a visual and viewpoint-motion reference. NASA footage, textures, simulation data, and source code are not included, and no NASA endorsement is claimed.
