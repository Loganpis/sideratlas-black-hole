# Third-party assets and scientific attribution

The bundled assets retain their own licenses. No repository-wide license for the original project code or media is declared in this release.

## Latin Modern fonts

The six unmodified `fonts/lmroman*.otf` files are from Latin Modern 2.005, by Bogusław Jackowski and Janusz M. Nowacki, based on Donald E. Knuth's Computer Modern family. Copyright 2003–2021 by B. Jackowski and J. M. Nowacki on behalf of TeX Users Groups.

They are distributed under the [GUST Font License](fonts/GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt), based on the [LaTeX Project Public License](fonts/LPPL-1.3c.txt). The original [README](fonts/README), asset inventory, and complete upstream distribution links are in [FONT-SOURCES.md](fonts/FONT-SOURCES.md). The bundled subset is unmodified.

## STIX Two Math

`fonts/STIXTwoMath-Regular.otf` is an unmodified asset from the official STIX v2.13b171 release. Copyright 2001–2021 The STIX Fonts Project Authors; licensed under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](fonts/STIX-OFL.txt). The original [font log](fonts/STIX-FONTLOG.txt) and [upstream source links](fonts/FONT-SOURCES.md) are included.

## Scientific references and visual inspiration

The current model's complete bibliography and the role of each study are in [MODEL.md](MODEL.md). Papers are cited, not redistributed. The numerical implementation is original.

NASA Goddard / Jeremy Schnittman's [2019 black-hole visualization](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/13326/) is credited as a visual and viewpoint-motion reference. NASA footage, textures, simulation data, and source code are not included, and no NASA endorsement is claimed.
