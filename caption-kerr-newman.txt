NO.001 — THE BLACK HOLE
@sideratlas

Bends light into multiple images of its disk
Approaching gas appears brighter
Gravity redshifts light and delays its arrival
Inner orbits shear bright structures faster
Spin drags spacetime around the black hole
Electric charge changes spacetime and light paths

Kerr–Newman: a* = 0.9, q = 0.4
Aligned, prograde disk: disk and hole rotate in the same sense
a* = Jc/(GM²), q = Q/[M√(4πε₀G)]

HYPOTHETICAL LARGE CHARGE · NEUTRAL TEST DISK
q = 0.4 is a deliberate theoretical choice, not a typical astrophysical charge
Photons are neutral; charge affects their paths through spacetime curvature
This does not simulate the electromagnetic response of real accretion plasma

CALCULATED
Kerr–Newman light paths, disk intersections, shifts and relative photon delays
Neutral circular orbits, ISCO and a recomputed Page–Thorne mean flux
Retarded emission with I_obs = g⁴ I_em

ASSUMED
Opaque razor-thin equatorial disk, ISCO to 28 GM/c², zero inner stress
Identical isotropic faces; prescribed shearing brightness patches
Full 360° virtual scan; no mass, accretion rate or temperature assigned
One 8-second/800 GM/c³ constructed loop repeated FOUR times: 32 seconds
Orange is illustrative brightness, not true visible color
No fluid evolution, Lorentz forces, plasma response, plunging emission or returning-radiation heating
