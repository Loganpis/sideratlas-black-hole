NO.001 — THE BLACK HOLE
@sideratlas

Original relativistic model — Kerr–Newman: a* = 0.9, q = 0.4
Hypothetical large charge · Neutral test disk · Illustrative color
Monochrome version: total-light intensity

Bends light into multiple images of its disk
Approaching gas appears brighter
Gravity redshifts light and delays its arrival
Inner orbits shear bright structures faster
Spin drags spacetime around the black hole
Electric charge changes spacetime and light paths

I_obs = g⁴ I_em (bolometric intensity)
Δ = r² − 2r + a² + q²; G = M = c = 1
r_ISCO ≈ 1.58 r_g; r_g = GM/c²
a* = Jc/(GM²); q = Q/[M√(4πε₀G)]
Aligned, prograde disk: disk and hole spin in the same sense

CALCULATED
Light paths, disk images, shifts and relative delays
Neutral circular orbits, ISCO and Page–Thorne mean flux
Emission evaluated at the retarded source time

ASSUMED
Opaque razor-thin disk from ISCO to 28 r_g, zero inner stress, identical isotropic faces and prescribed shearing brightness patches
360° virtual scan; 8-second/800 GM/c³ constructed loop repeated four times
No mass, accretion rate or temperature assigned
Orange depicts brightness, not true visible color
q = 0.4 is theoretical, not a typical astrophysical charge
No fluid evolution, Lorentz forces, plasma response, plunging emission or returning-radiation heating
