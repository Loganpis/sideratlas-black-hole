NO.001 — THE BLACK HOLE
@sideratlas

Bends light into multiple images of its disk
Approaching gas appears brighter
Gravity redshifts light and delays its arrival
Inner orbits shear bright structures faster
Spin drags spacetime around the black hole

Original Kerr calculation: a* = Jc/(GM²) = +0.9
Chosen alignment: equatorial, prograde — disk and hole rotate in the same sense
The inner emitting edge is the ISCO, about 2.32 GM/c²

CALCULATED
Kerr light paths, disk intersections, source azimuths, gravitational/Doppler shifts and relative photon delays
Spin-dependent Page–Thorne mean brightness; I_obs = g⁴ I_em
Kerr orbital motion: Ω = 1/(r^(3/2)+a), in G=M=c=1

ASSUMED
Opaque razor-thin disk to 28 GM/c², zero ISCO stress, identical isotropic emission on both faces
Sheared streaks are prescribed brightness, not simulated magnetic turbulence
The view makes a full 360° virtual scan above and below the disk
The 8-second loop represents 800 GM/c³; repeating source births close it without altering orbital speeds
No physical mass or accretion rate is assigned

Orange is illustrative BRIGHTNESS, not true visible color
No disk thickness, fluid evolution, magnetic fields, plunging emission, corona or returning-radiation heating
NASA's non-rotating 2019 animation is the visual reference; no NASA footage or code is used
